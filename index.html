Meet the founder

Guidance for the heart and the life you’re building.

Adebanjo Moyosore is the founder of Relationship & Reality Consult Africa. Based in Lagos, Nigeria, he supports individuals and couples in Nigeria and the diaspora through relationship coaching and conversations about emotional healing.

He describes more than four years of professional experience and training in counseling, basic psychology, and parenting toddlers. As convener of the HEARTRUB Conference, he brings people together for conversations about relationships, dating, trauma, and emotional healing.
